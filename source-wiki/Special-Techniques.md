### AvatarState
The AvatarState is the most powerful ability that the Avatar can conjure, allowing them to channel knowledge of all previous Avatars and perform extraordinary feats of bending for a short period of time.

> Hold sneak to start focusing on your chakras. Left click when the timing is right to open them. Release sneak to activate the AvatarState. If you managed to open all 7 chakras, AvatarState''s duration is greatly increased.

<details><summary>Default Config</summary>

```hocon
cooldown=120000
duration=60000
transient-cooldown=30000
transient-duration=3000
```
</details>

#### Tips
Each elemental chakra will be automatically opened if you have at least one ability of that element bound. Therefore, if you use a varied preset, you'll only need to open the remaining 3 chakras making it much easier to unlock the full Avatar State potential!
